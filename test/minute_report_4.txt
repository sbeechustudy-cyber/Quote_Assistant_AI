Rheinmetall wants to build a new generation of trucks that shall be possible to steer remotely. Therefore, a fail-operational steering system with ADAS features needs to be constructed on an SoC with a micro processor and a micro controller.\nThe micro controller needs to fulfill ASIL-D requiremends. The micro controller needs to fulfill ASIL-B to realize a ASIL-D to ASIL-B decomposition.\nEthernet support and security features are required. secure diagnostics are required. An IDPS is required, as well as a switch and possibly MacSEC encryption of the whole Ethernet LAN.\nThe micro controller will likely be an TC397, and the micro processor possibly a Traveo II.\nThe steering system also needs to interface with supporting CAN devices, some of which may have very limited functionality, and even LIN slaves.
