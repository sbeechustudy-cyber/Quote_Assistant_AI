🎯 Purpose of the tool

The EB Products Quote Assistant is designed to be both simple and powerful, providing essential support to sales managers by enabling them to quickly and easily generate a price indication for the RTC products range: Tresos 8, Tresos 9, Zoneo, and Zentur.

⬆ Back to Top

⚙️ How It Works

Based on a meeting report, customer's email or simple key words, the Assistant analyzes the content and generates a price indication according to various criteria:

  • Type of company
  • Type of project
  • OEMs
  • The µC or SoC with its compiler and MCAL
  • Required ASIL level
  • Communication, security, update features, etc.

From the provided elements, the tool identifies the company within Salesforce, confirming the currency, sales region, and responsible account. It then determines the appropriate license type for the quotation and compares it with what is stated in the report. Currently, only upfront payment licenses are supported; royalty model support will be available in a future release.

Once the license is determined, the Assistant estimates porting effort for Tresos (Zentur and Zoneo are not yet supported). Using the µC/SoC, compiler, and MCAL triplet, it applies a T-shirt sizing approach (from extra-small = off-the-shelf to extra-large = completely new µC/SoC). This estimation is based on the Unified Hardware List, containing all supported and soon-to-be-supported configurations.

Later releases will also provide a timeline for porting different packages. Currently, Zoneo and Zentur hardware are not yet included.

After identifying the license and porting effort, the tool moves on to product item identification. It essentially does the shopping for you by compiling your product list, managing inter-package dependencies, ensuring consistency (no mutually exclusive packages), and including all related packages automatically.

📝 Quotation Generation

Once license and porting effort are determined, the tool compiles the product list automatically:

  • Manages inter-package dependencies
  • Ensures consistency (no mutually exclusive packages)
  • Includes all related packages

The quotation is then generated based on:

  • Product item list
  • Sales region and currency
  • License type and application (development, maintenance, cybersecurity, LTS, IP/QP, etc.)
  • Official price list

Data sources used:

  • Salesforce
  • Unified Hardware List
  • Latest Price List

🤖 AI Analyzer Tab

Paste your meeting report into the text field, then click Analyze Minute Report. The progress window shows analysis steps. You cannot stop the process once started—either let it complete or reload the page.

Steps include:

  • Report review
  • License type identification and comparison
  • Porting effort estimation (T-shirt sizing) based on µC/SoC, compiler, and MCAL

Once the report review is complete, the Assistant identifies the license type and compares it with the reported one, then evaluates the porting effort. Results are shown using green checks (supported), red crosses (unsupported, requires porting), or question marks (requires further validation).

Next, the tool evaluates the porting effort based on the µC, compiler, and MCAL information. Using the T-shirt sizing method, it provides two lists

  • Closest candidate configurations matching the client request
  • All supported configurations for the specified µC/SoC

Column indicators:

  • ✅ Green check: fully supported
  • ❌ Red cross: not supported, porting required
  • ❓ Question mark: potentially supported, requires verification

The last column shows the T-shirt size (effort estimate). At the bottom, product items relevant to the request are listed.

Note: The Clear Logs button clears the “Analysis” window.

💰️ Price Indication Tab

This tab shows the commercial proposal, summarizing the currency, sales region, and licenses.

A download link is available for exporting the quotation in CSV format for further refinement or adaptation. Currently, quantities are not supported, and product items are not duplicated (as is sometimes the case with Zoneo transceivers).

Note: Quantities are not yet supported. Items are not duplicated (e.g., Zoneo transceivers).
Note: The Clear List button clears the list.

📋 AI Responses Tab

This tab provides a detailed view of the assistant’s answers to the questions that shape the quotation. This is particularly useful for modifying answers and regenerating a new quotation without requiring a new report analysis—saving time and boosting efficiency. For example, you can test different ASIL levels or OEM types.

Key information is shown in bold. If missing, the assistant makes assumptions, indicated by “flag assumption”.

After editing, click Update Price Indication. The updated quotation will appear automatically in the Price Indication tab.

Initial Price Indication allows you to reload the latest Assistant's analysis and regenerate automatically the associated price indication.

💡 Recommendations for Use

  • Input format is flexible, no pattern or template but avoid implicit information. Be explicit and unambiguous to ensure the AI analysis is as accurate and complete as possible.
  • If the assistant misidentifies information from your input, then go to AI Responses tab, edit Assistant's answers, and regenerate a new price indication with Update Price Indication—no need to modify the original report and request a new analysis.
  • The tool uses our internal AI, based on Ollama and Mistra. As a result, its processing capacity is limited and cannot handle a high number of simultaneous requests.
  • Do not refresh your browser page—this will not speed things up but will instead overload the server unnecessarily. Be patient: analysis may take between 1–2 minutes, or up to 5+ minutes..
  • Validate the results carefully. Users remain responsible for shared information with his or her customer.
  • The tool is under development. Report feedback,requests or issues, see Troubleshooting section.
